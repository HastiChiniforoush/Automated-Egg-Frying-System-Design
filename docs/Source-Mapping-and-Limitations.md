## Ownership and sharing

The report p. 5 lists the following tasks under **Hasti Chiniforoush**: measurement-chain design, circuit schematics, electrical BOM, signal conditioning, sensor component selection, hardware validation planning, and DFMEA support.

The functional structure, mechanical CAD, and HMI were responsibilities of other team members. Those figures are provided as **optional team artifacts** and should be uploaded publicly only with permission and with team attribution. The extracted circuit image is from the original group report as well; confirm redistribution rights before publication.

Do not upload the original group report without consent and without removing student IDs and other private information. The portfolio report in this package is newly written and contains no student IDs.

## Engineering cautions

- Academic concept only; no working prototype or measured validation results are evidenced.
- The report contains conflicting electrical BOM references and hardware specifications; those are documented but not silently fixed.
- No source CAD project files or editable electrical schematic were found inside the supplied PDF; the circuit figure is a raster extract.
- Prices reflect the 2026 project report and have not been re-quoted.
